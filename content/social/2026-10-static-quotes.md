## How to use a post

| Slot | Job | What the image does | What the caption does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · morning | Hook / Capture | One line that stops a scroll. A myth, a date, or a number. | Names the mistake. No ask yet. |
| 2 · midday | Convert | The mechanism in one sentence. | Teaches the rule and points at one tool. |
| 3 · evening | Act | One next step. | Sends them to a tool or to request a free consult. |

Suggested test window, then keep whatever your insights show: about 8:00, 12:30, and 5:30 in the audience’s time zone. People in this age group still live on Facebook in the morning. The evening post is the one an adult child is likely to see after work.

## Design spec (one template, three roles)

- Size: **1080 × 1350** (4:5). It takes more of a phone screen than a square.
- Safe zone: keep the quote inside the center. Leave about 120px clear at the top and bottom so buttons and the caption don’t cover the line.
- Type: one sentence, about 8–14 words. If it needs a second sentence, it belongs in the caption.
- Colors already on the site: ink `#0c2430`, paper `#eef3f1`, teal `#1f6f63`, amber `#d9912f`.
- Hook cards: ink type on paper, amber rule.
- Convert cards: teal label, ink type.
- Act cards: ink background, paper type, one line in amber.
- Small footer on every card: `FreeMedicareTools.com`
- Do not put a URL block, a phone number, a Medicare card, or a government seal on the image.
- Do not use stars, “best,” “#1,” or a made-up testimonial.

## Caption rules

Paste this closer on every post, after the teaching lines:

> Educational only. This site does not sign you up for a plan. Not affiliated with the U.S. government, CMS, or Medicare. Not legal, tax, or official benefits advice.

Act posts use one call to action: **Request a free consult** (`hello@freemedicaretools.com`) or a single tool link. Do not ask people to send drug names, a Medicare number, or a date of birth in a comment or a direct message.

Words kept off these cards on purpose: “enroll with us,” “guaranteed savings,” “$0 premium,” “free Medicare,” “best plan,” “quotes” (say “plan options”). Dollar figures below are 2026 CMS educational figures already used on the site. Penalties and IRMAA depend on the person’s own history.

## Compliance pass before anyone designs these

- No superlatives, no star ratings, no invented client stories.
- “Free” is never used for a premium, a benefit, or Medicare itself. The consult line is “Request a free consult,” matching the site.
- Dollar amounts are the 2026 figures already published on the site (Part B premium $202.90, Part D base beneficiary premium $38.99, IRMAA income lines).
- Each Act post has one job. Comments are not a place to collect personal information.
- A carrier compliance reviewer should still see these before they are posted as marketing, if your upline requires that. Educational posts that name plan benefits, gifts, or a specific carrier’s product are a different category and are not in this file.
